Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a My Number card to send money from Japan?

Yes for most bank transfers. Wise and Revolut require identity verification which includes My Number.

What is the cheapest way to send money from Japan?

Depends on the amount. For small amounts (<¥50,000), SBI Remit or Western Union via Seven Bank. For larger amounts, Wise typically offers the best value due to mid-market rates.

Can I send money from a convenience store?

Yes, SBI Remit supports convenience store payments. Western Union works through Seven Bank ATMs available at 7-Eleven stores 24/7.

How much money can I send from Japan per year?

There is no strict annual limit, but transfers over ¥1,000,000 require reporting to the Bank of Japan. Individual services may have their own limits.

Is it safe to use online money transfer services?

Yes, services like Wise, Revolut, and SBI Remit are regulated and licensed. Always verify you're using the official website. Check our Scam Checker tab if you're unsure.

How long does an international transfer take?

Typically 0-5 business days depending on the service and destination. Wise and SBI Remit are usually fastest (same day to 2 days). Bank transfers (SWIFT) take 3-7 days.

What fees should I watch out for?

Transfer fee, exchange rate markup, and intermediary bank fees. Our comparison tool shows the first two. Intermediary fees (¥2,000-5,000) may apply for SWIFT transfers.

Can I send money from Japan without a bank account?

Western Union via Seven Bank ATM doesn't require a Japanese bank account. PayPal only requires a PayPal account.
